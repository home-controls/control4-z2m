# Code of conduct

control4-z2m is a small project run by volunteers. Everyone who takes part (in issues, pull requests,
Discussions, and the project's thread on the Home Assistant Community forum) is expected to make it a
welcoming, harassment-free place for anyone, whatever their age, body, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity or expression, level of experience, education, nationality, appearance,
race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with people new to Zigbee, Home Assistant or Control4 hardware.
- Assume good faith; disagree about the work, not the person.
- Give and take criticism constructively, and own your mistakes.
- Respect people's privacy: don't post others' personal details, and keep names of people and rooms out of
  logs you share (CONTRIBUTING.md).
- Stay on topic, and keep the focus on what's best for the people who use the project.

## What isn't acceptable

- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Trolling, insults, derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Harassment, in public or in private.
- Publishing someone's private information (an address, an email, their home network details) without
  their explicit permission.
- Anything else that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Reporting

Report a problem privately to the maintainers through this repository's **Security** tab: **Report a
vulnerability**, with a title starting "Conduct:". That reaches only the maintainers. Say what happened,
where (links help), and when. Reports are handled in confidence, and the person reporting is protected
from retaliation.

## What the maintainers may do

Maintainers will review every report and may, depending on what happened:

1. **Correct:** a private note explaining what was wrong, and maybe a request for a public apology.
2. **Warn:** a warning with consequences for continued behaviour, and no interaction with the people
   involved for a while.
3. **Ban temporarily:** no interaction with the project for a set time.
4. **Ban permanently:** for a pattern of violations, harassment, or aggression towards anyone.

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that don't
follow this code.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces, and when someone represents the project elsewhere.
